# App Friends — brand brief

## The name

**App Friends.** Apps that help each other out. The relationship between two
non-competing apps that promote each other is a *friendship*; we lean into that
warmth without being saccharine.

## Positioning

The cross-promotion network for indie and mid-size app developers. You already
have users; so does the app down the street that doesn't compete with you. Trade
attention instead of buying it. When you do want to buy it, buy it directly from
developers you trust — no opaque ad exchange in the middle.

One line: **Trade installs with apps that aren't your competition.**

## Audience

App developers — solo indies to small studios — shipping on the App Store and
Google Play. They're allergic to ad-network jargon and CPM roulette. They like
SDKs that are three lines and a screenshot.

## Voice

- Plainspoken, developer-to-developer. Like a good README.
- Concrete. "Show a popup in three lines" beats "powerful integration."
- Numbers over adjectives. "12 friends, 40k impressions traded" not "huge reach."
- Cooperative, never extractive. The network works because it's balanced.

### Banned words

revolutionary, empower, unlock, leverage, seamless, delightful, supercharge,
game-changing, effortless, next-level, robust, cutting-edge, synergy.

### Word choices

- **Friends / pairing** for the free, reciprocal product.
- **Sponsored / campaign / advertiser / publisher** for the paid product.
- **Promo** = a single unit shown to a user. **Placement** = where it shows
  (popup, full-screen, banner).
- "Show" and "trade," not "serve" and "monetize" (except in billing contexts).

## Type

- Display: **Newsreader** (warm serif) for big headlines.
- Sans: **Geist** for UI and body.
- Mono: **Geist Mono** for code, bundle ids, API keys, stats.
- Tabular numerals in all dashboards and stat tables.

## Logo

Two overlapping rounded app-tiles (a violet one and a coral one) with a small
link/handshake notch where they overlap — two apps becoming friends. Wordmark in
Geist, "App" regular, "Friends" medium.

## Motion

Subtle. Popup springs in from scale 0.96 + fade. Full-screen slides up. Honor
`prefers-reduced-motion`.
